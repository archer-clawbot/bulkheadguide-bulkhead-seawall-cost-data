# Findings: Bulkhead and Seawall Cost Register (US, 2026)

All figures are as published; no figures were computed except ratios noted as such. Version 2026-10-21.

- **Vinyl per linear foot rises by market:** Shore Protect baseline $200–$450 (quoted TX jobs $260–$360); Docks of the Bay (Northern Neck VA) $300–$500; Atlantic Harbor (Broward FL) $400–$600; BluTide (Charleston SC) $450–$800 with timber cap, $550–$950 with concrete cap.
- **Material ladder (BluTide, Charleston):** treated timber $350–$650, composite $600–$1,100, concrete panel $700–$1,400, steel sheet pile $800–$1,600 per LF.
- **Aggregators sit lower than coastal contractors:** HomeGuide $150–$600/LF (national average project $35,000), HomeAdvisor $100–$800/LF (avg project $25,000). HomeGuide puts commercial/high-erosion work at $700–$2,000/LF.
- **Real project figures:** $348/LF quoted vinyl wall, Lake Olympia TX (125 LF, $43,500, no demo, no backfill); $231/LF signed riprap contract, Vicksburg MI ($43,805); $646/LF wood-to-vinyl replacement a Brick Township NJ resident reported paying ($51,700).
- **Repair:** Angi and HomeGuide $100–$250/LF; Shore Protect $120–$350/LF; Angi's leaning-wall fix $200–$600/LF.
- **Add-ons:** demolition $50–$150/LF (Calvert MD); permitting & engineering $2,000–$7,500+ (Broward FL); engineered plans $2,000–$3,000 (Calvert MD).
